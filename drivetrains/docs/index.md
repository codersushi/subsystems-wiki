# VEX IQ Drivetrains Guide

Welcome to the **Subsystems Wiki** drivetrain manual. Every concept here is **non-season-specific** and applies to any VEX IQ competition game.

## What This Guide Covers
* **Gear Ratios:** How to calculate speed vs. torque and choose the right ratio for your robot's weight.
* **Wheels & Omni Setups:** Where to place traction wheels vs. omni wheels so your robot turns smoothly without drifting.
* **Friction & Bracing:** How to prevent bent axles, motor stalling, and chassis flex.
* **Drivetrain Layouts:** Step-by-step breakdowns, build videos, and interactive 3D CAD models.
